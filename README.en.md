# WorkBuddy for Everyday Office Work

[简体中文](README.md) · English overview

<p><img src="assets/branding/mali-avatar-v1.svg" width="48" height="48" alt="Ma Li" align="absmiddle"> <strong>Ma Li</strong></p>

A Chinese guide for office workers who know how to use a computer but are new to WorkBuddy and AI agents. Learn to turn work notes into weekly reports, draft notices, combine spreadsheets and prepare presentations—with clear steps for checking, revising and reusing the results. No programming experience is required.

**[Read the complete Chinese book for free](全书.md)** · [Choose by task](manuscript/choose.md) · [Practice materials](examples/README.md)

October 2026 revision · 20 chapters in two parts · 28 teaching illustrations · 13 original exercise sets

Read online without cloning the repository or learning Git. For a first exercise, try [turning eight work notes into a weekly report](manuscript/first-file.md) (Chinese). WorkBuddy account requirements and pricing are separate from access to this book.

![WorkBuddy for everyday office work — seven habits to reduce repetitive tasks. By Ma Li.](assets/social/cover-bilingual-20261005.png)

The seven workflows cover learning from information, writing documents, analyzing tables, reporting progress, comparing solutions, coordinating work, and reusing recurring processes. A final project combines several methods and tests how they hold up when the inputs change.

This is an **English overview, not a full English translation**. The manuscript and exercises are in Chinese; no PDF edition is included. Product instructions draw on official documentation. Four interface drawings use official screenshots as their visual evidence; 24 concept and workflow diagrams explain the methods. The exercises are original fictional materials with author-written reference answers. This edition does not include hands-on client validation or measured productivity claims. See [sources and scope](资料与版本说明.md) in Chinese.
